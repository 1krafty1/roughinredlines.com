What Crew does

One job, one set of prints, everyone on it.

Everything in Rough-In Redlines, shared. No more emailing PDFs back and forth or wondering which copy has the latest markups.

Shared projects

One project for the office and the field.

The PM sets up the job once. The foreman opens the same project on the iPad, with the same sheets in the same order.

Everything syncs

Markups, notes, photos, counts and forms.

What the field marks up shows up on the office PC, and what the office adds shows up on the iPad. Windows and iPad, both ways.

Drawing sets & forms

Upload a set, or add a fillable form.

Drop in the drawing set for the job, or add fillable forms the crew fills in on site and the office gets back filled in.

Roles

The right access for each person.

Decide who runs the project, who marks up and who only looks.

OwnerAdminEditorViewer

Company sign-in

Sign in with your company email.

Use your work email and you join your company automatically. No codes to pass around the jobsite.

Share links

Send a link to people without the app.

The GC, the owner's rep or an inspector opens a share link in the browser. No app, no account.

Office ↔ Field

Marked up in the field. On the PM's screen in the office.

The foreman redlines on the iPad. The PM sees it on the Windows PC, adds a note and uploads the next revision. Everybody works from the same drawings.

Syncs both ways

Works offline on site

Basements, mechanical rooms, trailers with no signal. Keep marking up, and Crew syncs when you're back online.

Optional Procore link

Already on Procore? Link it if you want to. Crew works fine without it.

FAQ

Questions about Crew.

When does Crew launch?

Soon. We're finishing it now and piloting it with a few crews first. There's no date yet; get notified and you'll hear the day it's ready.

Do I need Procore?

No. Crew works on its own. If your company already uses Procore, the Procore link is there if you want it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Mark up on site with no signal, in basements and trailers. Crew syncs your changes when you're back online.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, as a pilot: we'll set your team up free on a real job so you can see if it works for you before you pay anything. Email us with your company and how many people.

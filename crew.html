What Crew does

One job, one set of prints, everyone on it.

Everything in Rough-In Redlines, shared. No more emailing PDFs back and forth or wondering which copy has the latest markups.

Shared projects

One project for the office and the field.

The PM sets up the job once. The foreman opens the same project on the iPad or iPhone, with the same sheets in the same order.

Everything syncs

Markups, counts, photos, notes and comments.

What the field marks up shows up on the office PC while they draw, and what the office adds shows up on the iPad. Windows, iPad and iPhone, every way.

Drawing sets & forms

Upload a set, or add a fillable form.

Drop in the drawing set for the job, or add fillable forms the crew fills in on site and the office gets back filled in.

Roles

The right access for each person.

Owners and admins run the company account. On each project, decide who manages it, who marks up and who only looks.

OwnerAdminProject managerEditorViewer

Company sign-in

Sign in with your company email.

Enter your work email, type the 6-digit code we email you, and you join your company automatically. No passwords, and no invite codes to pass around the jobsite.

Share links

Send a link to people without the app.

The GC, the owner's rep or an inspector opens a share link in the browser. No app, no account. Add a PIN, an end date or a download limit, and revoke it any time.

Office ↔ Field

Marked up in the field. On the PM's screen in the office.

The foreman redlines on the iPad or the phone. The PM sees it on the Windows PC, adds a note and uploads the next revision. Everybody works from the same drawings.

Syncs both ways

Works offline on site

Basements, mechanical rooms, trailers with no signal. Keep marking up, and Crew syncs when you're back online.

Optional Procore link

Already on Procore? Link it if you want to. Crew works fine without it.

Start Crew · on your own

Subscribe in the app, in about two minutes.

For a foreman, a PM or a one-person shop. Your company doesn't need to buy anything.

  1. Sign in to Crew

    Open Rough-In Redlines on iPhone or iPad (App Store) or on Windows and go to Crew. Enter your work email, tap Email me a code and type the 6-digit code. You join your company, or create it.

  2. Subscribe

    On iPhone or iPad tap Subscribe…, then Subscribe — $15.00/month (the App Store shows your local price), and confirm with your Apple Account. On Windows click Subscribe — $15/month, pay on the checkout page in your browser, then I've paid — check again.

  3. Work

    Open a project and mark up. Your subscription covers you on up to 3 devices: sign in on the others with the same email. Status and devices: Settings → Subscription (iPhone/iPad) or the account menu → Crew subscription… (Windows).

Start Crew · for a company

Buy licenses once, assign them to your people.

The company's Crew owner buys and assigns licenses from Rough-In Redlines on Windows. Everyone else just signs in.

  1. Sign in to Crew

    On a Windows PC open Rough-In Redlines → Crew and sign in with your company email. Create your company if it isn't there yet: it starts with a free 7-day trial for up to 4 people.

  2. Start Crew

    Click Start Crew — $15 per person per month, enter how many licenses you need and pay on the secure checkout page that opens in your browser.

  3. Assign licenses

    Your company's ⋯ menu → Licenses…: assign each license to a person by email and set Devices per license (1–5). Buy licenses adds more; Manage billing changes the card or cancels.

On iPhone and iPad your crew signs in and uses the license you assign; licenses are assigned on Windows. Someone with both a license and their own subscription pays twice: cancel the one you don't need.

FAQ

Questions about Crew.

Is Crew available now?

Yes. Crew launched with Rough-In Redlines 2.1 on November 14, 2026: on iPhone and iPad from the App Store, and on Windows from the Microsoft Store or this website. On Android tablets the app is on Google Play without Crew for now; Crew on Android comes later.

What does Crew include?

Shared company projects with drawing sets, forms, markups, counts, photos, notes and comments that sync live; sign-in with an emailed code; roles; share links; and Rough-In Pro (unlimited drawings) on your devices while your subscription or license is active. If you already bought Rough-In Pro, it keeps working.

Personal subscription or company licenses?

Either one lets a person work in Crew. Subscribe yourself in the app ($15.00/month on iPhone/iPad through the App Store, CA$19.00 in Canada; $15/month on Windows), or have your company buy licenses ($15 per person per month) and assign one to you. You don't need both.

How many devices?

A personal subscription covers you on up to 3 devices (any mix of PC, iPad and phone). A company license covers 1 to 5 devices, as the owner sets in Licenses…. Adding one more asks you to remove a device first.

What happens if a subscription or plan ends?

Nothing is deleted. Crew projects stay there to view, but you can't change them until you subscribe again or get a license. Changes made on the device while view-only wait there and go through when you're covered again.

How do I cancel?

iPhone/iPad: Manage subscription in the app, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on the device. Windows: Manage billing in the app, or the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt. You keep Crew to the end of the month you paid for. Deleting your Crew account does not cancel billing — cancel first.

Do I need Procore?

No. Crew works on its own.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Mark up on site with no signal, in basements and trailers. Crew syncs your changes when you're back online.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new company gets 7 days free for up to 4 people. Afterwards everyone keeps view-only access, and anyone with a subscription or license keeps working.

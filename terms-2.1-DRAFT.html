Summary (what Crew is, in plain words)

  • Crew is $15 per person per month: US $14.99 through Apple on iPhone and iPad; $15 through Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record) on Windows. It renews automatically each month until cancelled.
  • One subscription covers one person on up to 3 devices and includes Rough-In Pro.
  • A company can instead buy named licenses (1–5 devices per license) and assign them to people.
  • Cancel through Apple (Manage subscription) or Lemon Squeezy (Manage billing); access continues to the end of the paid month.
  • When a plan or subscription lapses, the company's projects become view-only for people without an active subscription. Nothing is deleted.
  • Deleting a Crew account does not cancel billing; the app warns subscribers to cancel first.

Part A — new EULA section: "Crew subscription"

Crew lets people share drawings, markups, comments and assignments with the people in their company through our online service. Using Crew requires a Crew account (sign-in by email) and, except as described below, a paid subscription.

1. What you buy. A Crew subscription is a personal subscription for one person's Crew account. It covers that account on up to 3 devices at a time (any mix of iPhone, iPad and Windows PC), in every company the person belongs to, and includes Rough-In Pro on those devices while it is active. It can't be shared with or transferred to another person. A company may instead buy company licenses for named people under a separate order or checkout (Section 4 below).

2. Price and billing.

  • iPhone and iPad: US $14.99 per month, sold by Apple as an in-app purchase and charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions also apply.
  • Windows (website and Microsoft Store editions): US $15.00 per month, sold through our reseller and merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy, under its payment, tax and refund terms.
  • Prices exclude taxes unless shown otherwise. Prices outside the United States are set by the store in local currency.
  • Price changes: we may change the price for future billing periods. Apple notifies subscribers of increases and, where its rules require, asks for consent before renewing at the new price; for Lemon Squeezy subscriptions we give at least 30 days' notice before a new price applies to your next renewal. [Attorney] confirm notice periods.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. YOUR SUBSCRIPTION RENEWS AUTOMATICALLY EVERY MONTH AT THE THEN-CURRENT PRICE UNTIL YOU CANCEL IT. On iPhone and iPad, Apple charges your Apple Account for the renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the current period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before then. Lemon Squeezy charges your payment method at the start of each billing period.

4. How to cancel. You can cancel at any time; you don't need to contact us.

  • Apple: in the app, Manage subscription, or on your iPhone/iPad in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Rough-In Crew → Cancel Subscription. We can't cancel an Apple subscription for you.
  • Lemon Squeezy (Windows): in the app, Manage billing, or from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for; then it ends.

5. Refunds.

  • Apple: refunds are handled by Apple under its policies (reportaproblem.apple.com). We can't issue refunds for Apple purchases. If Apple refunds a purchase, the subscription ends immediately.
  • Lemon Squeezy: refund requests go to support@roughinredlines.com or Lemon Squeezy. [Decide] refund policy (e.g. none for partial months, except where required by law). A refunded subscription ends when it is refunded.
  • Except as required by law or by the store's policy, payments are non-refundable and we don't give credits for partial periods.

6. Payment problems. If a renewal payment fails, the store may retry for a period (Apple: a billing grace period of up to 16 days during which Crew keeps working, then up to 60 days of retries without access; Lemon Squeezy: about 2 weeks of retries during which Crew keeps working). If payment isn't resolved, the subscription ends.

7. When a subscription ends: VIEW-ONLY, NOTHING DELETED BY THE ENDING. When your subscription ends (cancelled, expired, refunded or payment not resolved) and no company license covers you:

  • You can still sign in and view the projects, drawings and markups of companies you belong to, but you can't change them (no new markups, uploads, share links or other edits) until you subscribe again or a company license covers you. Changes you make on a device while you are view-only stay on that device and are sent when you have an active subscription again.
  • Your own drawings and markups stored only on your device keep working, but Rough-In Pro features unlocked by Crew stop (the free tier's limits apply again) unless you bought Rough-In Pro separately.
  • Ending a subscription does not by itself delete anything from Crew. [Attorney] see Part C — this replaces the 30-day read-only-then-delete rule drafted in EULA v1.1 DRAFT Section 21 for personal subscriptions.

8. Company trials and licenses. A new company gets Crew free for 7 days for up to 4 people; no payment is needed and nobody needs a subscription during the trial. Afterwards, each person needs a personal subscription or a company license to make changes; everyone keeps view-only access. A company that buys company licenses (Lemon Squeezy or invoice) assigns each license to a named person; each license covers 1–5 devices as the company sets. A person may have both a personal subscription and a company license: they are separate charges, nothing is merged or credited automatically, and each must be cancelled separately.

9. Devices. A subscription covers up to 3 devices at a time. Adding a fourth asks you to remove one. We may limit or suspend use that looks like account sharing.

10. DELETING YOUR CREW ACCOUNT DOES NOT CANCEL YOUR SUBSCRIPTION. You can delete your Crew account in the app (Delete account…). Deleting the account removes your account and its link to your subscription, but Apple or Lemon Squeezy will keep charging you until you cancel the subscription with them (Section 4). Cancel first.

11. Restoring and changing accounts. A subscription belongs to the Crew account that bought it (Apple records that account in the purchase). Restoring purchases on a different Crew account doesn't move it; if the original Crew account was deleted, the subscription may be claimed by a new Crew account you sign in with.

12. Family Sharing. Crew subscriptions are not shared through Apple Family Sharing.

13. Changes to Crew. We may add, change or remove Crew features. If we discontinue Crew, we will stop renewals and give at least 30 days' notice; [Decide] pro-rated refund of the current period for Lemon Squeezy subscribers.

14. Apple. For purchases on iPhone and iPad, Apple is not responsible for Crew or its content and has no obligation to provide support or maintenance for it; the Apple-specific terms already in Section 14 of this Agreement apply.

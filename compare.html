Honest fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't.

A good fit if you…

  • Are an electrician, plumber, HVAC or low-voltage tech, foreman or estimator who lives on the prints
  • Want to redline, count devices, measure and send it back without a subscription
  • Work in places with no signal, and want your drawings to stay on your device
  • Send markups to an office that uses Bluebeam or Acrobat
  • Are a PM who needs to sign, redact, combine and print, not run a whole platform

Probably not for you if you…

  • Need CAD: Rough-In marks up PDFs and pictures, it doesn't draw or edit DWG files
  • Need a plan room or document-control platform with RFIs, submittals and transmittals
  • Run detailed estimating takeoffs with cost databases and bid reports
  • Need a large, live multi-company review session today (Crew, our team version, is still coming soon)
  • Need it on an iPad, Mac or Android tablet today: Windows is out now, the rest is on the way

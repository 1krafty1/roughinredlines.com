Rough-In Redlines 2.1

What's new in 2.1

Crew for your whole company, share links, any color with the APWA utility palette, color legends, a much better Find & Count, and cleaner Bluebeam exports. On iPhone, iPad and Windows; Android tablets on Google Play.

Step-by-step instructions for everything below are in the 2.1 user manual (PDF), chapter "New in 2.1".

Crew: your whole company on the same drawings

  • Shared company projects: drawing sets, forms, markups, counts, photos, notes and comments, synced live between iPhone, iPad and Windows. Works offline and catches up when you're back online.
  • Sign in with your work email and a 6-digit code we email you. No passwords.
  • Roles: company owner and admin; on each project, project manager, editor or viewer.
  • $15 per person per month, Rough-In Pro included. Subscribe yourself in the app ($14.99/month through Apple on iPhone and iPad, $15/month through Lemon Squeezy on Windows; one subscription covers you on up to 3 devices), or your company buys named licenses (1–5 devices each) and assigns them in Licenses… on Windows.
  • If a subscription or plan ends, Crew projects become view-only. Nothing is deleted.

Share links

  • Share files from Home or Crew, or Share link… / Share link with markups… on a drawing or set. Anyone with the link downloads the files in a browser, no app or account.
  • Options: a PIN (4–6 digits, sent separately), how long it works, and a download limit (one-time, limited or unlimited).
  • Shared links lists every link: see downloads, Extend it, or Revoke it at once.
  • Send as one PDF: a whole set goes as one PDF (the original file itself when there are no markups).
  • File size: Actual size, Smaller (good for email) or Smallest. Only photos and scanned pictures shrink; linework, text and markups stay sharp, and your drawing isn't changed.

Share links are part of Crew.

One Add button

Everywhere you bring something in (Home, a project, Crew's Add drawings, Share files) there's one Add button with the same three choices: Photos, Take photo and Files.

Any color, the APWA palette, fills and color legends

  • Any color for any markup: More colors… opens a color wheel, a hex box, your recent colors and My colors (your own palette, with what each color means).
  • The APWA uniform color code built in: white (proposed excavation), pink (survey), red (electric), yellow (gas and oil), orange (communications), blue (potable water), purple (reclaimed water and irrigation) and green (sewer and drain).
  • Fills for areas, polygons, boxes, ellipses and clouds, with a fill color and transparency; turn the outline on or off and set its weight.
  • Polygon in Shapes: a plain shaded polygon, no scale needed. On an area, Show area turns the area label on or off.
  • Color legend: insert a legend box listing the colors with their meanings, optionally only colors used on this sheet. It prints and exports; Show color legends hides them all. Color key shows the palette's meanings on screen.

Snapshots and pasted pictures

  • A border around a pasted snapshot: select it, turn Border on, pick Thin, Medium or Heavy; the color swatch sets the border color. Settings can border every new snapshot.
  • Arrows, clouds, notes and pen marks drawn on a pasted picture stay on top of it. Send to back and Bring to front change the order; the eraser leaves the picture alone.

A much better Find & Count

  • Reads tag text, so tags with different letters are told apart.
  • Finds symbols at any angle, and with pipes, centerlines or gridlines running through them.
  • A progress bar with matches joining the list as they're found; Cancel keeps what was found.
  • Works in a Crew set on the sheet you're looking at.

Cleaner Bluebeam exports

  • Counts arrive as Bluebeam Count Measurements (iPad and Windows), so they can be counted, moved and edited in Revu.
  • Highlights stay see-through highlights when moved or recolored in Bluebeam.
  • Polygons and filled shapes arrive as Bluebeam polygons with their fill and transparency.

Where it runs

  • iPhone and iPad (and Macs with Apple silicon): the App Store.
  • Windows 10 and 11: the Microsoft Store, or the installer from this website.
  • Android tablets: Google Play. Crew on Android comes later.

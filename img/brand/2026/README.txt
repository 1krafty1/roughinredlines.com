ROUGH-IN REDLINES - BRAND KIT (October 2026)
Logo design: Julie. Kit built from her 1254 px JPEG (no source file exists).

THE MARK
A silver R next to a red R whose stem is an up-arrow, whose bowl is a duplex
receptacle symbol and whose leg is a down-arrow, in a glowing white-to-red ring
on a dark disc with faint blueprint linework.

Colours
  Red (logo, glows, rules)     #FF3A1E   Red letter gradient  #FF5A38 -> #E8371C
  Red for buttons/fills        #D42F17   (white text on it passes WCAG AA)
  Red for text on charcoal     #FF6A4D
  Print red (light version)    #D93A22
  Silver                       #F4F5F7 -> #BCC1C7      Graphite (light version) #2B3036
  Charcoal backgrounds         #08090C  #0E0F13  #15171C
Fonts (website/social): Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, narrowed to 82% width (headlines),
  Geist (body), Geist Mono (small caps labels). All free on Google Fonts.

WHICH FILE TO USE WHERE
vector/ (SVG: sharp at any size; first choice for print shops, signs, decals, shirts)
  rr-logo-2026.svg                 Full logo on its square dark background (Julie's composition)
  rr-logo-2026-transparent.svg     Full logo, transparent outside the ring: place on any dark surface
  rr-mark-2026-letters.svg         Just the two R letters, transparent: overlays on video/photos
  rr-logo-2026-light.svg           For white paper/pages: graphite R + red R, thin graphite/red ring, no dark disc
  rr-mark-2026-letters-light.svg   Letters only for white backgrounds
  (The glow and smoke use SVG filters. Browsers and Inkscape render them; some
   print/vinyl software ignores filters. For vinyl/embroidery use the *-light or
   *-letters files, which are flat colour.)

png/ (exported from the vector at 512, 1024, 2048, 4096 px)
  rr-logo-2026-<size>.png                    Full logo, dark square: slides, documents, app stores' "logo" fields
  rr-logo-2026-transparent-<size>.png        Full logo, transparent corners
  rr-mark-2026-letters-transparent-<size>.png  Letters only, transparent: video overlays, watermarks
  rr-logo-2026-light-<size>.png              Light-background version (transparent PNG): letterhead, invoices, white web pages
  rr-mark-2026-letters-light-<size>.png      Letters only for light backgrounds

master/ (Julie's artwork itself, with her texture, enlarged)

RULES OF THUMB
- Dark backgrounds are home. On white, use the *-light files.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least 1/4 of its width.
- Don't recolour the letters, stretch, rotate, or put the glowing version on busy photos
  (use the letters-only file for that).
- Under 64 px always use the small/ files.
- App icons are a separate decision and were not changed.

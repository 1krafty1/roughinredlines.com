How the estimate works

  • Time back = people × hours a week × 50 working weeks × the share of time saved. In dollars, × the labor rate.
  • Printing = sets a month × cost per set × 12 × the share of printing you'd stop.
  • The share of time saved is an assumption, not a promise: set it to what you believe.
  • Crew costs $15 per person a month, every year, and includes Rough-In Pro. Rough-In Pro is $39.99 once per person (a Windows key works on 2 PCs; Pro on the iPad or Mac and on Windows are bought separately), then nothing.
  • Pays for itself in = the cost ÷ the savings per day. Savings for each $1 = savings ÷ cost.

We haven't used any industry statistics here: the examples are round numbers to start from. Free covers 3 drawings, so you can time a real markup before you buy anything.

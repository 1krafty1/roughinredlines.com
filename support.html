Getting started

  • Open a drawing from Files, or share a PDF from Mail to Rough-In.
  • No PDF handy? Tap Try a Sample Drawing on the home screen.
  • Pick a tool along the bottom: Pen, Highlight, Text, Symbols, Count, Measure, White-out or Photo.

Common questions

How do I open a PDF?
From the home screen tap Open and choose it in Files, or in Mail tap the attachment, then Share → Rough-In. Pictures (JPG, PNG, iPhone photos) open the same way.
Apple Pencil or finger?
With a Pencil, the Pencil draws and your finger moves the sheet. No Pencil? Turn on Finger draws in the toolbar; then two fingers move the sheet.
How do I send markups to someone who uses Bluebeam?
Tap Send PDF → PDF for Bluebeam / Acrobat. Every markup stays editable there, with your initials. For a simple read-only copy use Flattened PDF.
How do backup and restore work?
Settings → Backup → Choose Backup Folder, then pick a folder in iCloud Drive or Dropbox. Every drawing you change is copied there with its markups. On a new iPad, Settings → Restore from Backup brings them back, still editable.
How do I compare two revisions?
Open the new drawing, then More → Compare with Older Print and pick the old one. Use Overlay, Side by Side or Blink; Cloud Changes clouds every difference and Bring Markups copies your old markups over.
How do panel schedules work?
In a drawing, More → Panel Schedules. Add a panel, tap a circuit to describe it, Select → Combine for 2- and 3-pole breakers, then Send to get an Excel file and a PDF card. Put your company name and logo in Settings → Company profile.
Does it run on a Mac?
It will, on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), when it launches on the App Store (coming soon). It's the same app and the same download as on the iPad, and Rough-In Pro bought on your iPad unlocks it on your Mac too (Restore Purchases, same Apple Account). Draw with the mouse or trackpad; pinch or press ⌘= / ⌘- to zoom. On an Intel Mac, use the Windows version.
Is there a Windows version?
Yes, on Windows 10 and 11: get it free from the Microsoft Store. It's free for 3 drawings, and Rough-In Pro for Windows is $39.99 once, for 2 PCs. Panel schedules are iPad-only for now. See How buying works on Windows below.
Does it work without signal?
Yes. Everything works offline. Sending and backup wait until you're connected.

Where does my license key arrive?
By email, from Lemon Squeezy, to the address you used at checkout, usually within a minute or two. Don't see it? Check spam or promotions, then email support@roughinredlines.com with that address and we'll resend it.
How do I activate Pro?
Open Rough-In Redlines on the PC, click Unlock Pro, paste the key from the email and activate. The PC needs an internet connection for that one step; after that Pro works offline.
Can I use it on more than one PC?
Yes, one key works on 2 PCs at a time, for example the office desktop and the trailer laptop.
How do I move my key to a new PC?
On the old PC, choose Deactivate this PC. That frees the slot, so you can paste the same key into Unlock Pro on the new one. Old PC already gone or dead? Email us your key and we'll free it for you.
Microsoft Store or the installer: does it matter?
No. It's the same app either way, and the same key unlocks Pro whether you installed it from the Microsoft Store or with the Windows installer, and whether you bought Pro on this site or from Unlock Pro inside the app.

No, they're bought separately: Windows through Lemon Squeezy with a license key, and the iPad, iPhone and Mac app (coming soon) through the App Store.

Rough-In Redlines

Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Operated by RK Technology LLC (Randy Kraft), North Dakota

Rough-In Redlines is an iPad, iPhone, Mac and Windows app for marking up PDF drawings. It is built so your drawings and markups stay with you. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

  • The drawings (PDFs and pictures) you open, every markup you make, photos you add, panel schedules, counts and settings are stored on your iPad, iPhone or Mac (or your PC, for the Windows version).
  • The app has no account and no server. We don't receive your drawings, markups or photos.

When something leaves your device

  • When you send or save it. Exports (PDFs, spreadsheets, editable copies) go only where you send them, using Apple's share sheet or Files: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, a folder you choose, and so on.
  • Backup, if you turn it on. Backup copies your drawings to a folder you choose in your own storage (for example iCloud Drive or Dropbox). That storage is run by its provider under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo or open a picture. Photos stay with the drawing on your device unless you send or back it up.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location data.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Purchases

If the app offers paid features, purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Rough-In Redlines for Windows

  • Drawings, markups, photos, palettes and settings are stored on your PC, inside the app's own data folder. There is no account and no server; we don't receive your drawings.
  • License key. When you activate Rough-In Pro, the app sends your license key and a label for the PC (for example "Windows PC 7Q2K") to Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, which checks the key. The app re-checks it when it starts with an internet connection, and frees the activation when you choose Deactivate this PC.
  • Updates. The version downloaded from this website checks for a newer version only when you click Check for updates (one request to roughinredlines.com, which sends nothing about you). The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store.
  • Purchases. Rough-In Pro for Windows is sold by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record), in your web browser, under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Children

Rough-In Redlines is a work tool for the construction trades and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

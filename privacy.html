Rough-In Redlines

Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Operated by RK Technology LLC (Randy Kraft), North Dakota

Rough-In Redlines is an iPad, iPhone, Mac, Windows and Android tablet app for marking up PDF drawings. It is built so your drawings and markups stay with you. Version 2.1 adds Crew, an optional online service for sharing projects with the people in your company. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes and why.

Using the app without Crew

  • The drawings (PDFs and pictures) you open, every markup you make, photos you add, panel schedules, counts and settings are stored on your iPad, iPhone or Mac (or your PC or Android tablet, for those versions).
  • Without a Crew sign-in there is no account and no server. We don't receive your drawings, markups or photos.

When something leaves your device

  • When you send or save it. Exports (PDFs, spreadsheets, editable copies) go only where you send them, using Apple's share sheet or Files: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, a folder you choose, and so on.
  • Backup, if you turn it on. Backup copies your drawings to a folder you choose in your own storage (for example iCloud Drive or Dropbox). That storage is run by its provider under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Crew, if you sign in. What you add to a Crew project is stored on the Crew service, as described below.

Crew (shared projects)

Crew is optional and works on iPad, iPhone, Mac and Windows. When you sign in to Crew, the following is stored on the Crew service so the people in your company can share it:

  • Account: your email address, a display name and initials, and a random account ID. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password.
  • Company and role: the companies you belong to and your role in each company and on each project (for example owner, admin, manager, editor or viewer). A company can add its name and logo, and can claim its email domain so that people who sign in with an address there join it.
  • Project content: the drawing sets and sheets uploaded to Crew projects, and the markups, counts, notes pages, comments, assignments and photos added to them, with who made each change and when. Photos added to markups and items are re-saved by the app before they are uploaded, so no camera location data is kept.
  • Share links: if you send files by link, the files, the link's title and note, when it expires, its download limit, its PIN if you set one (stored only in scrambled form), and a log of each download with a shortened network address (for example 203.0.113.0/24) and the browser type (for example "Safari on iPhone"). Files uploaded for a link are removed from the service within about an hour after it expires or is revoked; files from a Crew project stay in the project.
  • Devices and alerts: a random ID for the app on each device, the platform and app version, and, if you allow notifications, the device's push token, so Crew alerts (such as a new revision or an assignment) reach you. Your notification settings are stored too, and Crew can send you alert emails.
  • License Agreement: which version of the License Agreement you accepted, when, and in which app and on which platform.

Who can see it. The people on a Crew project see its content, and who made each change. A company's Crew owners and admins control who is in the company and on each project. RK Technology LLC can access the data only as needed to run and support the service.

Crew data is used only to provide Crew. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to track you.

Crew subscriptions and purchases

  • Who bills you. On iPhone and iPad, Apple processes Crew subscription payments. On Windows, Lemon Squeezy (our reseller and merchant of record) does. We never receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address; the store keeps those.
  • What the Crew service keeps about your subscription (linked to your Crew account): the store (Apple or Lemon Squeezy), the product (Crew Monthly), the store's subscription and transaction IDs (Apple's original transaction ID; Lemon Squeezy's subscription ID and the email address on it), the status (for example active, cancelled, in a billing grace period, expired or refunded), when the current period ends, whether it will renew, whether there is a payment problem, whether the purchase was real or in Apple's test sandbox, the devices it covers (a random device ID, the platform and a name such as "iPad"), and the notices the stores send us about it (renewals, cancellations, refunds), kept as a record of each change.
  • Why. To know whether your Crew account may use Crew on a device, to show you your subscription's status, and for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and handling disputes.
  • Apple. When you subscribe, the purchase carries your Crew account's random ID, so our service can link the subscription to your account. The app sends the purchase record Apple signed to our service to confirm it, and Apple sends our service notices about renewals, cancellations and refunds (App Store Server Notifications). Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.
  • Company licenses. If your company assigns you a Crew license, we keep the assignment (your email address, the company and the devices it covers) and show it to the company's owners and admins.
  • How long. While your Crew account exists. If you delete your Crew account, your subscription record and its devices are deleted from the service; the notices the stores sent us are kept as billing records for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons. Deleting your Crew account doesn't cancel the subscription itself: cancel it with Apple or Lemon Squeezy (see the Terms).

Service providers

  • Supabase (the Crew database, file storage and sign-in), hosted in the United States.
  • Resend sends the sign-in codes, invitations and Crew alert emails.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases, sends App Store Server Notifications and delivers push alerts on iPad, iPhone and Mac (Apple Push Notification service).
  • Lemon Squeezy processes payments for Rough-In Pro and Crew on Windows.

They process data only to provide their services to us. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to Crew data is limited by company, project and role.

How long Crew data is kept

  • Crew data is kept while your company uses Crew. When a project is deleted, its content is removed from the service.
  • Nothing is deleted because a subscription, a license or a company's plan ends.
  • When you sign out on a device, that account's Crew drawings and data are removed from the device. Your own drawings on the device stay.
  • If your company stops using Crew, its data is deleted on request, unless we are required by law to keep it.

Deleting your Crew account

You can delete your Crew account in the app: Settings → Crew account → Delete account… (on Windows, the same item). The app emails a code to your address to confirm. Deleting removes your sign-in, your company memberships, your personal layers and their markups, your devices, your subscription record and your notification settings. Markups, items and comments you made on shared project layers stay with the company and show as "Former member". Records you accepted (such as the License Agreement) are kept without your name. Your own drawings on your device are not touched. If you are the only owner of a company with other people in it, you are asked to make someone else owner first.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information at support@roughinredlines.com.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo or open a picture. Photos stay with the drawing on your device unless you send it, back it up, or add it to a Crew sheet.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location data.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

This website

roughinredlines.com counts visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics: which pages are viewed, the referring site, and the country. It uses no cookies, doesn't store your IP address, and builds no profile of you. This applies to the website only; the app itself has no analytics.

Purchases

Rough-In Pro on iPad, iPhone and Mac is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active. Crew subscriptions are described above.

Rough-In Redlines for Windows

  • Drawings, markups, photos, palettes and settings are stored on your PC, inside the app's own data folder. Without a Crew sign-in there is no account and no server; we don't receive your drawings. If you sign in to Crew on Windows, the same Crew information described above is stored on the Crew service.
  • License key. When you activate Rough-In Pro, the app sends your license key and a label for the PC (for example "Windows PC 7Q2K") to Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, which checks the key. The app re-checks it when it starts with an internet connection, and frees the activation when you choose Deactivate this PC.
  • Updates. The version downloaded from this website checks for a newer version only when you click Check for updates (one request to roughinredlines.com, which sends nothing about you). The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store.
  • Purchases. Rough-In Pro and Crew for Windows are sold by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record), in your web browser, under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Rough-In Redlines for Android

  • Drawings, markups, photos, palettes and settings are stored on your tablet, inside the app's own storage. There is no account and no server; we don't receive your drawings.
  • Files and photos. The app opens only the PDFs and pictures you pick or share to it with Android's own file picker. It asks for no camera, photo-library, location or contacts permission.
  • Purchases. Rough-In Pro for Android is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing, under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details; Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is active.
  • Updates come from the Google Play Store.

Children

Rough-In Redlines is a work tool for the construction trades and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and Crew users will be told of significant changes.

BEI Redline

Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Published by RK Technology LLC (Randy Kraft), North Dakota, for Bergstrom Electric

BEI Redline is an iPad, iPhone, Mac and Windows app that Bergstrom Electric crews use to mark up PDF drawings. It is built on Rough-In Redlines. Version 2.0 adds BEI Crew, shared projects for the office and the field. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes and why.

Using the app without Crew

  • The drawings (PDFs and pictures) you open, every markup you make, photos you add, panel schedules, counts and settings are stored on your device.
  • Without a Crew sign-in there is no account, and nobody, including the app's publisher, receives your drawings, markups or photos through the app.
  • When you send or save something. Exports (PDFs, spreadsheets, editable copies) go only where you send them, using the share sheet or Files: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, a folder you choose, and so on.
  • Backup, if you turn it on. Backup copies your drawings to a folder you choose (for example iCloud Drive, OneDrive or a company folder). That storage is run by its provider under its own privacy policy.

BEI Crew (shared projects)

Crew is optional. When you sign in to Crew with your Bergstrom email, the following is stored on the Crew service so the people on your projects can share it:

  • Account: your email address, a display name and initials (taken from your email), and a random account ID. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password.
  • Company and role: the company you belong to (Bergstrom Electric) and your role in it and on each project (for example owner, admin, manager, editor or viewer).
  • Project content: the drawing sets and sheets uploaded to Crew projects, and the markups, counts, notes pages, comments, punch-list items and photos added to them, with who made each change and when. Photos are re-encoded when they are uploaded, so no camera location data is kept.
  • Devices and alerts: a random ID for the app on each device, the app version, and, if you allow notifications, the device's push token, so Crew alerts (such as a new revision) reach you. Your notification settings are stored too.

Who can see it. The people on a Crew project see its content, and who made each change. Bergstrom Electric's Crew owners and admins control who is in the company and on each project. RK Technology LLC can access the data only as needed to run and support the service.

Crew data is used only to provide Crew. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to track you.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), hosted in the United States.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code and Crew alert emails.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) delivers push alerts on iPad, iPhone and Mac.

They process data only to provide their services to us. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited by company, project and role.

How long Crew data is kept

  • Crew data is kept while Bergstrom Electric uses Crew. When a project is deleted, its content is removed from the service.
  • When you sign out on a device, that account's Crew drawings and data are removed from the device. Your own drawings on the device stay.
  • If Bergstrom Electric stops using Crew, its data is deleted on request, unless we are required by law to keep it.

Deleting your Crew account

You can delete your Crew account in the app: Settings → Crew account → Delete account… (on Windows, the same item). The app emails a code to your address to confirm. Deleting removes your sign-in, your company memberships, your personal layers and their markups, your devices and your notification settings. Markups, items and comments you made on shared project layers stay with the company and show as “Former member”. Records you accepted (such as the License Agreement) are kept without your name. Your own drawings on your device are not touched. If you are the only owner of a company with other people in it, you are asked to make someone else owner first.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information at support@roughinredlines.com.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo or open a picture. Photos stay with the drawing on your device unless you send it, back it up, or add it to a Crew sheet.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location data.
  • No purchases. BEI Redline is free.
  • Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising.

BEI Redline for Windows

Drawings, markups, photos, palettes and settings are stored on the PC, inside the app's own data folder. There is no license key. If you sign in to BEI Crew on Windows, the same Crew information described above is stored on the Crew service.

Children

BEI Redline is a work tool for electrical crews and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and Crew users will be told of significant changes.

Rough-In Redlines is an app for marking up PDF drawings on iPad, iPhone, Mac, Windows and Android tablets. It's built so your drawings stay with you unless you choose to share them. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes and why.

1. Using the app on your own (no Crew)

Drawings, markups, photos, panel schedules, counts, palettes and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, and we don't receive your drawings, markups or photos. Exports go only where you send them. If you turn on Backup, copies go to storage you choose (for example iCloud Drive or Dropbox), under that provider's own policy.

2. Crew (team projects)

Crew is optional. If you or your company use it, we store the following on our service so your team can share it:

  • Account: your email address and name, the company you belong to, and your role. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email; we don't store passwords.
  • Project content: the drawings, forms, markups, notes, photos, counts and form values added to Crew projects, plus who made each change and when.
  • Activity and notifications: assignments, notification settings, and device tokens for push notifications if you allow them.
  • Share links: when someone opens a share link, we record the time, a partial IP address and the browser type, to count downloads and prevent misuse.
  • Procore (optional): if you connect Procore, we store the connection securely to read your projects' drawings and, if you choose, send markups back. You can disconnect at any time.

Your company's owners and admins control who is in the company and on each project. The people on a project can see its content.

Who sees it. The sender, the people on that project in the sender's company, the other people who received the same request, and RK Technology LLC as needed to run and support the service. We use service providers to host data (Supabase, United States) and send email (Resend). We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it. Approval records, including the drawings as approved, are kept permanently, even if the sender later deletes the project. The record is evidence of an agreement between you and the sender, and both of you may need it. Other data, such as unopened or expired requests, is deleted after [X] days.

Security. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to each request needs its own link and PIN, and approval records are digitally sealed so changes can be detected.

Your choices. You can ask the sender for a copy or correction, or contact support@roughinredlines.com. We may be unable to delete a completed approval record because it is needed as a record of an agreement; we'll tell you if so.

4. Purchases, subscriptions and licenses

  • iPad, iPhone, Mac: handled by Apple. We only learn whether a purchase is active.
  • Android: handled by Google Play. We only learn whether a purchase is active.
  • Windows: sold by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). When you activate Pro, the app sends your license key and a label for the PC (for example "Windows PC 7Q2K") to Lemon Squeezy to check the key. We never see your payment details.
  • License agreement: when you accept the License Agreement, the app stores the version and date on your device, and on our service if you're signed in to Crew.

Crew subscriptions

  • Who bills you: on iPhone and iPad, Apple processes Crew subscription payments; on Windows, Lemon Squeezy (our reseller and merchant of record) does. We never receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address — the store keeps those.
  • What our service keeps about your subscription (linked to your Crew account): the store (Apple or Lemon Squeezy), the product (e.g. Crew Monthly), the store's subscription and transaction identifiers (Apple's original transaction ID; Lemon Squeezy's subscription and customer IDs), the status (active, cancelled, in a billing grace period, expired, refunded), the current period's end date, whether it will renew, whether there's a payment problem, the purchase environment (production or Apple's test sandbox), the devices it covers (a random device ID, the platform and a device name such as "iPad"), and the notifications the stores send us about it (renewals, cancellations, refunds), kept as records of each change.
  • Why: to know whether your Crew account may use Crew on a device, to show you your subscription's status, and for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and dispute handling.
  • How long: while your account exists, and afterwards as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons [Decide/Attorney: e.g. 7 years for billing records]. If you delete your Crew account, the link between your account and the subscription is deleted; store records needed for those purposes are kept without your account.
  • Apple: the app reads its own signed purchase records from Apple on your device and sends them to our service to confirm the purchase; Apple also sends our service notices about renewals and refunds (App Store Server Notifications). Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.
  • Company licenses: if your company assigns you a license, we keep the assignment (your email, the company, the devices it covers) and show it to the company's owners and admins.

5. Updates

The Windows version downloaded from our website checks roughinredlines.com for updates and sends nothing about you. Store versions are updated by their store.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in, hosted in the United States) and Resend (email delivery), plus Apple (in-app purchases, App Store Server Notifications, push notifications), Google, Microsoft and Lemon Squeezy (payments, Windows) as described above. They process data only to provide their services to us.

7. What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking in the app, and no location data. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

8. How long we keep data

Crew data is kept while your company uses Crew. When a project is deleted, its content is removed within [30] days, except completed approval records (section 3). When your company stops using Crew, its data is deleted within [90] days of a request, unless we must keep it by law. Share-link and sign-in records are kept for [12] months. A lapsed plan or subscription by itself deletes nothing: projects become view-only.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by company, project and role. Approval records are digitally sealed. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as required by law.

10. Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your Crew profile in the app, leave a company, delete your Crew account in the app (Settings → Crew account → Delete account…; this does not cancel a subscription — cancel it with Apple or Lemon Squeezy first), or ask us to access, correct or delete your information at support@roughinredlines.com. Your company's admins can also remove your content from their projects. We'll respond within [45] days. We may keep information we're required to keep, such as completed approval records.

11. This website

roughinredlines.com counts visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics (pages viewed, the referring site, country). It uses no cookies and doesn't store your IP address.

12. Children

Rough-In Redlines is a work tool for the construction trades and isn't directed to children under 13.

13. Changes and contact

We'll post changes here with a new effective date and tell Crew users of significant changes. RK Technology LLC · support@roughinredlines.com

Built for the jobsite

Everything you do to a print, minus the paper.

Made for foremen working with an Apple Pencil, a finger, or gloves on, in basements and trailers with no signal.

01 · Markup

Redline fast, with real trade symbols.

Pen, highlighter, clouds, arrows and text, plus 600+ symbols across 17 trade libraries. Size a symbol once, then Copy & Place it across the sheet.

  • Saved pens for one-tap color and width
  • Highlighter with adjustable opacity
  • Stamps with your initials and date

02 · Compare

New revision? See what changed.

Pick the old print and Rough-In lines up every sheet by its number. Removed work shows red, new work blue.

  • Overlay, side by side, or blink
  • A list of every change, including text like "20A → 30A"
  • Cloud every change, then bring your old markups over

03 · Panel schedules

Directory cards without Excel on the iPad.

Odd circuits left, even right, just like the card on the panel door. Type or tap descriptions, combine 2- and 3-pole breakers, mark spares.

  • Excel and PDF cards with your company name and logo
  • Open an existing schedule and keep going
  • Room numbers straight off the drawing

04 · Send

Send it back the way the office wants it.

One menu, whatever the office uses. Send only the marked-up pages of a big set.

How it works

Three steps, no laptop.

  1. Open the print

    From Files, or share the PDF straight out of Mail. Photos and pictures open too.

  2. Mark it up

    Redline, place symbols, count, measure, and add photos right where the work is.

  3. Send it back

    A Bluebeam-ready PDF, a flattened copy, counts for the estimator or panel cards for the PM.

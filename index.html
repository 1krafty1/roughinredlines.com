How it works

Three steps, no laptop.

  1. Open the print

    From Files, or share the PDF straight out of Mail. Photos, pictures and password-protected PDFs open too.

  2. Mark it up

    Redline, place symbols, count, measure, sign, and add photos right where the work is.

  3. Send it back

    A Bluebeam-ready PDF, a flattened copy, a plot, counts for the estimator or panel cards for the PM.

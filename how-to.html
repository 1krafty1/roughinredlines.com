01 · 35 s

Open a drawing and get around

Open a PDF set, zoom, scroll through the sheets, jump to a sheet and switch between drawings with tabs.

  1. Click Open file and pick a PDF
  2. Hold Ctrl and roll the wheel to zoom
  3. Fit the sheet to the window
  4. Roll the wheel to scroll through the sheets
  5. Click "Sheet … of 4" to go to a sheet
  6. Open another drawing — it gets its own tab
  7. Click a tab to switch drawings

02 · 47 s

Redline basics

The everyday markups: pen, revision clouds and shapes, text notes, white-out with a fill color, and undo/redo.

  1. Pick Pen, a color and a width
  2. Draw on the sheet
  3. Shapes → Cloud: drag around the change
  4. Text: click the sheet and type a note
  5. White-out: pick a fill, drag a box
  6. Made a mistake? Undo (Ctrl+Z)
  7. Redo (Ctrl+Y) brings it back

Customize your tools and toolbar

Settings → Tools: Simple markup for the office or every tool for the trades, single tools on and off, and the toolbar on any side of the window.

  1. Tools → Settings…, then the Tools section
  2. Simple: just the markup tools, for the office
  3. Trades: every tool, for the field
  4. Or switch single tools on and off
  5. View → Toolbar position: Top, Left, Right or Bottom
  6. Or drag the toolbar by its grip

Place symbols, drop copies with Copy & Place, make your own palette, capture a symbol off the drawing and import a Bluebeam tool chest (.btx).

Stamps & signatures

Stamp with your initials and the date, draw your signature once and sign tickets and forms with the date and your printed name.

  1. Stamps: pick a stamp
  2. Click the sheet — adds your initials and the date
  3. New signature: draw it once

08 · 34 s

Redact

Search & redact names, phone numbers, emails and prices, box anything else, then make a redacted copy or redact the drawing itself. The text is removed, not just covered.

  1. Open Redact on the toolbar
  2. Search & redact: type a name, tick what to find
  3. Click Find — every match gets a box
  4. Or drag a box over anything else
  5. Apply redactions…
  6. Make a redacted copy, or redact this drawing
  7. The copy opens — the text under the boxes is gone

Count and Find & Count

Count by clicking, or box one symbol and let Find & Count mark every match on the sheet.

  1. Count → New count, give it a name
  2. Click each one on the print
  3. Click a mark again to take it off
  4. Find & Count: box one symbol
  5. Click Find — it marks every match
  6. Click any that don't belong, then Create count

Scale & measure

Use the scale printed on the sheet, measure distances and runs, or calibrate from a known dimension.

  1. Pick Measure — it reads the scale off the sheet
  2. Click Use it
  3. Click two points to measure
  4. Run: click each point, double-click to finish
  5. No scale on the sheet? Calibrate

Compare two revisions

Compare a new print with the old one: changes are listed and marked, check them side by side or with Blink, cloud them and bring your old markups over.

In the Pages view: drag pages into order, rotate them, undo, and extract pages into a new drawing.

Print to a printer or plotter

Pick the pages and the paper (Letter to Arch E), print at actual size, half size or fit to paper, with auto-rotate, then choose your printer or plotter.

  1. Click Print (or press Ctrl+P)
  2. Print this sheet, all sheets, or marked-up ones
  3. Pick the paper: Letter up to Arch E
  4. Actual size, Half size or Fit to paper
  5. Auto-rotate turns each sheet to fit the paper
  6. Continue → pick your printer or plotter in Windows

Send to the office

Export a PDF whose markups stay editable in Bluebeam and Acrobat, a flattened PDF, the counts as a CSV, or the whole job as a .redline file.

14 · 36 s

Move drawings between iPad and PC

Save a .redline file on the PC, open it on the iPad, mark up in the field and bring it back, with every markup and count.

  1. On the PC: Save → .redline file
  2. It holds the drawing and every markup
  3. Send it to the iPad: email, OneDrive, Teams…
  4. On the iPad: mark up, then Share → .redline file
  5. Back on the PC: Open file and pick the .redline
  6. Already on this PC? Replace it, or keep both
  7. Markups, counts and photos come along

15 · 34 s

Unlock Rough-In Pro & move your license

Free for 3 drawings; a one-time license key unlocks unlimited drawings. Activate it, and deactivate a PC to move the key to a new one.

  1. Free: every tool, on up to 3 drawings
  2. Click the chip — Buy opens the store, the key comes by email
  3. Paste the license key, click Activate
  4. Pro: unlimited drawings on this PC, works offline
  5. Moving to a new PC? Settings → Rough-In Pro
  6. Deactivate this PC, then enter the key on the new one

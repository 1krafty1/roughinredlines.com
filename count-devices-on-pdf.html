How to count devices on a PDF drawing

Receptacles, fixtures, data drops, smoke detectors: somebody has to count them, and a missed device shows up later as a short order. Here are three ways to count on a PDF, from the old way to the fast one, and how to check the number before you trust it.

Way 1: the highlighter and a tally

Print the sheet, run a highlighter over every duplex, and keep a tally in the margin. It works, and most of us learned this way. The trouble is that it's slow, it's easy to lose your place on a busy sheet, and when the next revision comes out you start over. On a PDF you can do the same thing faster and keep the result.

Way 2: count marks

Count marks are the digital tally. Each tap drops a numbered mark on the device and adds one to the total.

  • Start a count. Pick Count, then New count, and give it a name like "Duplex receptacles" or "2x4 troffers".
  • Tap each device. Every one gets a numbered mark. Tap a mark again to take it off.
  • Work in a pattern. Go room by room, or left to right in strips, so nothing gets skipped.
  • Use one count per device type. Separate counts keep separate totals, by sheet and for the whole set.

Count marks are the right call on a small sheet, on a scanned print that's hard to read, or when the symbols aren't drawn consistently.

Way 3: Find & Count

On a clean CAD sheet, let the app do the looking. In Rough-In Redlines, Find & Count finds every copy of a symbol you box. It's on the iPad and on Windows.

  • Box one symbol. Open Find & Count and drag a tight box around one symbol on the plan.
  • Pick where to look. This sheet or all sheets. On Windows you can also pick certain pages.
  • Check the matches. Every match is highlighted and listed by sheet. Tap a row to zoom to it, and untick anything that isn't the device.
  • Adjust the Sensitivity slider. Strict finds fewer, closer matches; Loose finds more. It filters right away, and your unticks are kept.
  • Create count. The kept matches become numbered count marks, in a new count or one you already have. Spots you already counted are skipped.

Tips for a total you can trust

  • Box the symbol tightly. Keep the box close around the symbol. Extra wall lines, text or a circuit number in the box make it look for those too.
  • Box one on the plan, not in the legend. Legend symbols are often drawn bigger than the ones on the floor plan. Box a typical device in a room so the size matches.
  • Watch for look-alikes. A duplex, a GFCI and a quad can differ by a letter or one line. Run them as separate searches, check the results, and untick the ones that belong to another count.
  • Scan the sheet after. Zoom out and look for a device with no mark: one under a note or a dimension line, or one turned at an odd angle.
  • Count each revision. When a new set comes out, compare it to the old one to see what moved, then re-run the count on the new sheets.

Measure the runs while you're there

Counts usually come with footage. Pick Measure and Rough-In reads the scale off the sheet; tap Use it and measure distances and runs in feet and inches. No scale printed, or the sheet says NTS? Calibrate off a dimension you know. See Scale & measure.

Get the totals to the estimator

  • Counts CSV. Totals by sheet and for the set, plus runs, in a file that opens in Excel.
  • Count page in the PDF. The flattened PDF adds a page with the totals, so the counts travel with the drawing.
  • PDF for Bluebeam. Count marks stay editable for an office that uses Bluebeam or Acrobat. From the Windows app they arrive as Bluebeam Count measurements, totaled in Bluebeam. More on that in A Bluebeam alternative for electricians.

Questions foremen ask

Can it count symbols on every sheet at once?
Yes. Find & Count can search the sheet you're on or every sheet in the set, and lists the matches by sheet. On Windows you can also pick certain pages.
Why did Find & Count miss some devices?
Usually the box was loose, or it was drawn around the legend symbol, which is often bigger than the ones on the plan. Box one device on the plan tightly and try a looser Sensitivity setting, then add any stragglers by tapping them with the Count tool.
Can I get the counts into Excel?
Yes. Export the Counts CSV for totals by sheet and for the set. The flattened PDF also gets a count page.
Does it work on scanned drawings?
Find & Count works best on clean CAD sheets. On a rough scan, use count marks and tap each device; the totals work the same way.
